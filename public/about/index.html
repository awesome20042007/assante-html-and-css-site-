Who We Are

About Asante Church International

Our History

Founded in 2014 by the late Pr. Christopher Habineza. The church is now led by Pr. Elijah Willingiye, Pr. Fred Kutesa, and Mummy Betty Habineza.

Our Mission

To identify, equip and release God's people into their destiny through His word, the power of the Holy Spirit, ministering, deliverance, divine guidance and blessings in all areas of life.

Our Vision

To build a Christ-centered community that equips believers to walk in victories and fulfill God's purpose.

Core Values

  • Divine Calling and Obedience — Following God's anointed direction.
  • Faith and Courage — Moving forward in God's strength, even through challenges.
  • Provision and Stewardship — Recognizing and managing God's blessings responsibly.
  • Revelation and Wisdom — Seeking God's insight in all we do.
  • Spirit Empowerment — Fasting in the Holy Spirit to transform lives and communities.

Statement of Faith

1. The Word of God — We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible and authoritative word of God, sufficient for teaching, reproof, correction, and training in righteousness (2 Timothy 3:16-17).

2. The Godhead — We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, who are co-equal and co-eternal (Matthew 28:19).

3. Jesus Christ — We believe Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, fully man, who lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, was buried, and rose again the third day (1 Corinthians 15:3-4).

4. The Holy Spirit — We believe the Holy Spirit is present in the world to convict, regenerate, sanctify, and empower believers for service. The baptism in the Holy Spirit is available to all believers, accompanied by the initial evidence of speaking in tongues (Acts 1:8; Acts 2:4).

5. Salvation — We believe salvation is a free gift of God's grace received through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ, resulting in the forgiveness of sins and the new birth (Ephesians 2:8-9; John 3:3-5).

6. The Church — We believe the church is the body of Christ, called to worship God, make disciples, and manifest His love in the world. The local church is a community of baptized believers committed to fellowship, prayer and evangelism (1 Corinthians 12:27; Acts 2:42).

7. Baptism and Communion — We believe in water baptism by immersion for believers and the observance of the Lord's Supper as ordinances of the church, symbolizing Christ's death, burial, and resurrection (Matthew 28:19; 1 Corinthians 11:23-26).

8. Divine Healing — We believe in divine healing as provided in the atonement of Christ, accessible to believers through prayer and faith (Isaiah 53:5; James 5:14-15).

9. The Second Coming — We believe in the personal, visible, and imminent return of Jesus Christ to establish His eternal Kingdom. All believers will be resurrected and glorified (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17; Revelation 22:12-13).

10. Spiritual Gifts — We believe spiritual gifts — prophecy, healing, and miracles — are active today to strengthen the Church and advance God's Kingdom (Ephesians 4:12).
